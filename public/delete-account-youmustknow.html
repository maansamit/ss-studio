Delete Account - You Must Know

Users can request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to samitsam@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject Delete My You Must Know Account.
  3. Include the Google email address used to sign in.

What will be deleted?

  • Firebase Authentication account
  • Name
  • Email address
  • Associated quiz progress (if stored)

Data retention

Your request will normally be processed within 7 days.
