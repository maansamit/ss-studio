Privacy Policy - Digital Mandir

Last updated: May 28, 2026

Digital Mandir app aapki privacy ka poora dhyan rakhti hai.

1. Data Collection

Hum kisi bhi tarah ka sensitive personal data (jaise Location, Contacts, Photos, ya SMS) access ya store nahi karte hain. Hum sirf Firebase Authentication ke liye aapka mobile number use karte hain taaki aap login kar sakein.

2. Payment Security

Payment ke liye hum UPI services ka use karte hain. Hum aapka koi bhi bank detail ya card number apne server par store nahi karte.

3. Changes

Hum apni privacy policy mein kabhi bhi badlav kar sakte hain. Aap ise samay-samay par check karte rahein.
